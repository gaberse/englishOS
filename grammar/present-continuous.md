# Present continuous

**Rule:** subject + am/is/are + verb-ing — for actions happening right now
**Common mistake:** Forgetting the verb "to be" → "I wearing" instead of "I am wearing"
**Example correct:** ✓ _I am wearing a plain green shirt. She is running._
**Example wrong:** ✗ _I wearing a shirt. He wearing a jacket._
