# Prepositions of place

**Rule:** in = inside / on = surface / at = specific point / between = middle of two / next to = beside / behind = detras / in front of = delante
**Common mistake:** "stayed on my bed" → stayed IN my bed (you're inside it!)
**Example correct:** ✓ _My laptop is on the desk. Micha is next to the door. My phone is in front of me._
**Example wrong:** ✗ _Micha is in the door. My laptop is at the desk._
