# Does/do questions

**Rule:** Does/do takes the -s in questions — the main verb returns to base form.
**Common mistake:** "Does she has?" → ✗ — does already carries the he/she/it meaning
**Example correct:** ✓ _Does she have a jacket? Yes, she has a jacket._
**Example wrong:** ✗ _Does she has a jacket?_
