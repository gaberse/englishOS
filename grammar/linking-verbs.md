# Linking verbs

**Rule:** look / seem / feel / are — connect subject to description. Use instead of "are" for richer descriptions.
**Common mistake:** Using "are" for everything — "his sunglasses are nice" → better: "his sunglasses look really nice"
**Example correct:** ✓ _He seems like a happy person. His jacket feels warm._
**Example wrong:** ✗ _He is like a happy person. His jacket is warm._ (correct but flat)
