# Love / like / hate + -ing

**Rule:** After love/like/hate/enjoy/miss → second verb gets -ing. After want/need/plan → use "to".
**Common mistake:** "I hate go" → ✗ / "I loves to playing" → ✗
**Example correct:** ✓ _I love cuddling with my girlfriend. I hate going to the office._
**Example wrong:** ✗ _I hate go. I love to playing._

## note
love/like/hate also work with "to" — both are correct!
_I love running_ = _I love to run_ ✓
