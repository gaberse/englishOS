# Regular and irregular past verbs

**Rule:** Regular → add -ed. Irregular → memorize. No -s EVER in past tense (not even he/she/it).
**Common mistake:** "I buy a bikini" → I bought / "she comed" → she came
**Example correct:** ✓ _I stayed home. She came to visit. I bought a bikini for Mallorca._
**Example wrong:** ✗ _I buyed. She comed. He wents._

## key irregular verbs
go → went | come → came | become → became | give → gave | buy → bought | have → had | see → saw | eat → ate | wake → woke | sleep → slept
