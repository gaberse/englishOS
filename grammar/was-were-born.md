# was / were born

**Rule:** "born" always needs the verb to be before it. Never say "I born" alone.
**Common mistake:** dropping the verb to be
**Example correct:** ✓ _I was born in Venezuela._
**Example wrong:** ✗ _I born in Venezuela._
